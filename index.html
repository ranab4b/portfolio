Engineering Philosophy

Code quality isn't a checklist. It's the architecture.

Every project is built on principles that hold up under scale, team turnover, and changing requirements — not just principles that read well in a proposal.

  • Clean ArchitectureLayered boundaries that isolate business logic from frameworks and I/O.
  • SOLID PrinciplesCode that's easy to extend and hard to accidentally break.
  • Test-Driven DevelopmentTests written alongside logic, not bolted on after launch.
  • CI/CD PipelinesAutomated build, test, and release gates from commit to store.
  • Agentic AI-Driven WorkflowsAI-assisted tooling used to accelerate delivery without compromising review rigor.
